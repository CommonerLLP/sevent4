# Scope

The Unelected City owns a public static atlas for explaining who controls
everyday Indian city services and jurisdictions.

## The Unelected City owns

- Static public pages under `public/`.
- City-console generation, public map layer publication, and browser smoke QA.
- City-specific interpretation: readiness grades, public-service layers,
  jurisdiction crosswalks, finance pages, findings, and limitations.
- Source-policy rules that distinguish official evidence, open-data context,
  and unverified leads.

## Out Of Scope

- Generic crawling, retrieval, embedding, search, and MCP infrastructure.
- Private campaign infrastructure or voter-file workflows.
- Re-licensing third-party data under the code license.
- Treating every selectable console as publication-grade evidence.

Reusable acquisition or retrieval work belongs upstream in the CommonerLLP
shared repos named in `docsx/architecture.md`.
