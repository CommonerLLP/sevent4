The city bus, funded over two decades

The AMTS line is the corporation’s capital support to the bus undertaking (“Loan to AMTS”), the legible budget anchor each year. The 2026–27 book reframes this as “AMTS + BRTS = 3000 EV buses (GCC model).”

What the corporation tops up

Recent budget books (2018–2024) make several civic lines legible: the bus undertaking, BRTS/Janmarg, the school board top-up over the state grant, the V.S. Hospital grant, and the M.J. Library grant. These are the choices the elected corporation controls directly.

Sources & method

Figures are extracted from Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation budget books (English editions). Amounts are normalised to rupees crore; 1 crore = 100 lakh. Budget books carry several columns — prior-year actual, current-year revised estimate, and budget-year estimate — and each headline figure prefers the self-labelled narrative allocation. Figures are human-verified from the source PDFs and carry per-row confidence; they are a research reading of public documents, not an official account. Treat low-confidence and blank cells with caution.

AMTS 'Loan to AMTS' is a capital LOAN/grant-support line from the corporation fund to the transport undertaking; it is NOT the AMTS operating deficit. The AMTS operating deficit anchor (~Rs.200 cr/yr per the Drive FY23-24 summary) is a separate operational figure not isolated as a single line in these capital blocks.
