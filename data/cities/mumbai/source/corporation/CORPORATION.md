### Why elections were delayed for ~4 years (the reason)
After the term expired in March 2022, BMC (and ~all Maharashtra local bodies) was run by an **Administrator** rather than an elected body. Polls were stalled by litigation in the **Supreme Court over OBC reservation and ward delimitation** — the count/structure of wards (Maharashtra had moved to 236 then back) and the restoration of OBC reservation in local-body seats were contested. The **Supreme Court order of 6 May 2025** directed that pending Maharashtra local-body elections be held within four months, on the **pre-2022 ward structure (227 wards) with OBC reservation as it existed before 2022**. The State Election Commission then ran delimitation (mid-2025), notified the schedule on **15 December 2025**, and held polling on **15 January 2026**.
