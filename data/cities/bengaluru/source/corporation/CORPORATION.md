# Bengaluru — BBMP / Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA)

## HEADLINE: An 11-year democratic gap — Bengaluru has had no elected city council since 2020

**Bengaluru, India's third-largest city, has been run without any elected local council for the entire period since its last council's term expired — an unprecedented ~11-year gap (2015 election to the scheduled 2026 poll).** The city is governed by state-appointed administrators / IAS officers, not by elected corporators.

### Precise timeline

| Event | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Last municipal (BBMP) council election | **2015** | Held; council seated for 5-year term |
| Elected council term ended | **September 2020** | Council ceased; **administrator appointed** |
| Administrator-run from | **Sept 2020 onward** | No elected body since |
| Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024 introduced | 23 July 2024 | Karnataka Legislative Assembly |
| GBG Act passed (Assembly) | 10 March 2025 | |
| GBA established / notified | **15 May 2025** | BBMP structure replaced |
| GBA full operational capacity | 2 September 2025 | Chief Commissioner M. Maheshwara Rao (IAS) as administrator |
| First GBA / 5-corporation elections | **scheduled by August 2026** | First municipal poll since 2015 |

The 2015 BBMP council's term ending in 2020 with no successor election, the subsequent restructuring delays, ward-delimitation litigation, and the GBA reorganisation together produced an unbroken stretch of unelected, administrator-run governance. Even with the 2026 election, the new corporations are critiqued as having limited powers under the GBA superstructure (PIL pending in Karnataka High Court).

## GBA restructuring: BBMP dissolved into 5 corporations

The Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024 replaced the single Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP, 2007–2025) with an apex **Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA)** — chaired by the Chief Minister — sitting above **five new city corporations**, covering ~712 km².

| Corporation | Code | Wards | Area | Headquarters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru Central | BCCC | 63 | 78 km² | Hudson Circle |
| Bengaluru North | BNCC | 72 | 158 km² | Yelahanka |
| Bengaluru South | BSCC | 72 | 147 km² | Jayanagara |
| Bengaluru East | BECC | 50 | 168 km² | Mahadevapura |
| Bengaluru West | BWCC | 112 | 161 km² | Rajarajeshwari Nagara |
| **Total** | | **369** | **712 km²** | |

- Ward delimitation expanded the old 198 BBMP wards to 368, later +1 in BWCC = **369 wards**.
- Combined registered electorate cited at **88,92,528 voters**.
- The Karnataka State Election Commission (SEC) finalises electoral rolls ahead of the 2026 poll; the Supreme Court / Karnataka HC pressed for elections within set deadlines.

## Councillor roster

**There is NO current elected councillor (corporator) roster.** Bengaluru has been administrator-run since September 2020; no elected body exists to roster. The first elected corporators of the five new corporations will only be chosen in the ~August 2026 GBA elections.

Historical roster (2010–2015 / 2015–2020 BBMP corporators) is the last set of elected representatives but is not captured here; it would need to be labelled clearly as historical/expired if added later.
