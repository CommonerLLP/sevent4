# Governing Statutes — Mumbai (Maharashtra)

Authoritative legal texts that constitute and govern the administration of Greater
Mumbai / the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, assembled for the civic-accountability atlas.

**Primary source:** India Code (indiacode.nic.in), the Government of India's official
consolidated repository of enforced Central and State Acts. Each India Code PDF is a
maintained running text that carries a "[Text as on <date>]" stamp on its first page,
i.e. all amendments up to that date are already incorporated into the body text.

**Provenance note:** India Code's `bitstream` filenames are not stable/guessable — the
working download paths below were taken from the live `handle/...` landing pages, not
from search-engine snippets (several snippet URLs returned "Invalid URL"). All four
files were verified as real multi-page PDFs whose first-page headers match the act
short title, act number and year.

Downloaded: 2026-06-09.

## Recent amendments (2019–2024) — note

The four India Code texts are running consolidations, so amendments up to each file's
"Text as on" date are already folded into the body. Specifics relevant to Mumbai:

- **MMC Act, 1888:** Amended repeatedly through 2022–2024 (e.g. Maharashtra amending
  Acts of 2022, 2023 and 2024 touching ward composition/elections and administrative
  provisions). The downloaded text is current to **30 Dec 2025**, so these are
  incorporated. *Exact amending-act numbers for each change are unverified at the
  individual-section level and would need section-by-section confirmation against the
  Maharashtra Gazette / Law & Judiciary Dept. before being cited as authority.*
- **MMRDA Act, 1974:** Text current to **4 Apr 2024**.
- **MRTP Act, 1966:** Text current to **10 Mar 2025** (the MRTP Act has been among the
  most-amended Maharashtra statutes, including unified DCPR provisions).
- A separate **Maharashtra Metropolitan Region Development Authority Act, 2016**
  (2017 : Mah. III) exists as generic enabling law for *other* metropolitan regions; it
  is distinct from the Mumbai-specific 1974 Act and is **not** downloaded here (cite
  only: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19681/1/20173.pdf).

## Which body holds which 12th-Schedule function

The Constitution (74th Amendment) 12th Schedule lists 18 functions for urban local
bodies. In Mumbai these are split across statutes/bodies rather than resting wholly with
the municipal corporation:

- **MCGM / BMC (under the MMC Act, 1888)** — the bulk of day-to-day municipal functions:
  water supply; public health, sanitation, solid-waste management; drainage and
  sewerage; roads and bridges; street lighting; building regulation; fire services;
  burial grounds; markets; registration of births and deaths; public conveniences.
  **Urban planning including town planning** and **regulation of land-use and
  construction** are exercised by MCGM *as the Planning Authority for Greater Mumbai*,
  but **the powers and procedure flow from the MRTP Act, 1966** (development plan / DCPR
  / development permissions), not from the 1888 Act alone.
- **MMRDA (under the MMRDA Act, 1974)** — **planning for economic and social
  development** and large-scale **urban/regional infrastructure** at the metropolitan
  scale (the Regional Plan for MMR, mass-transit and trunk infrastructure, major
  projects). It operates region-wide across multiple municipal bodies and is the
  metropolitan-tier planning/execution authority.
- **MRTP Act, 1966** — not a "body" but the **enabling statute** that confers the
  town-planning, land-use-regulation and development-control functions and designates
  who the Planning Authority / Special Planning Authority is for a given area.
- **Electricity supply + urban transport** for the island city: discharged by the
  **BEST Undertaking**, a statutory undertaking of MCGM constituted *under the MMC Act,
  1888* (Brihan Mumbai Electric Supply & Transport Committee / Undertaking).
- A **Metropolitan Planning Committee** (Art. 243ZE) is the constitutional integrating
  body for the metropolitan area; in practice MMRDA's regional planning role overlaps
  heavily with it. *MPC constitution/effectiveness in MMR is a known accountability gap
  and is flagged as unverified here pending a dedicated source check.*
