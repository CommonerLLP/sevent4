## Notes on amendments & the Greater Chennai expansion

- **CMC Act 1919 — long amendment history.** The CAG consolidated PDF carries amendments through the 1990s+; the title was changed "Madras"→"Chennai" by the statewide renaming (TN Act 37 of 1996, eff. mid-1996). Citation should read "Chennai City Municipal Corporation Act, 1919 (TN Act IV of 1919)".
- **Greater Chennai expansion — correction to the brief's "2021–22" date.** The major territorial expansion that created today's **Greater Chennai Corporation (426 km², 200 wards, 15 zones)** occurred in **October 2011**, when 42 local bodies (9 municipalities incl. Ambattur, Alandur, Tiruvottiyur, Madhavaram, Maduravoyal, Valasaravakkam, etc., plus town panchayats and panchayat unions) were merged, more than doubling the old ~174 km²/155-ward city. This was effected by government notification under the 1919 Act, not a fresh statute. No further statutory expansion to 426→~800 km² had been enacted as of compilation (a residents'-body demand only). The "2021–22" framing in the task appears to conflate the 2011 expansion with the 2022 local-body elections; treat **2011** as the expansion year unless a later notification is located. *(Flagged as the one factual correction to the brief.)*
- **TNTCP 1971 — recent amendments.** The principal Act has NOT been repealed. Notable recent amendment: the **Tamil Nadu Town and Country Planning (Second Amendment) Act, 2022** (enacted 22 Jun 2022), which adds provisions for **Urban Development Authorities** for Urban Planning Areas. Amendment chain through TN Act 31 of 2018 is appended in the local PDF; the 2022 amendment is NOT yet incorporated in the downloaded consolidated copy — *cite only* for the 2022 amendment (source: legitquest / TN gazette) until a gazette PDF is sourced.
- **CMWSSB 1978 — latest incorporated amendment** in the downloaded text is the Second Amendment Act, 1997 (consolidation as-amended-up-to 30 Nov 1998). No 2019–2024 amendment confirmed; *unverified* whether later minor amendments exist.

## Which body holds which 12th-Schedule (74th Amendment) function

The Constitution's 12th Schedule lists 18 functions for urban local bodies, but in Chennai several are **parastatal-held**, not exercised by the Corporation:

- **Water supply (domestic, industrial, commercial) + sewerage / sewage treatment** → **CMWSSB / Chennai Metrowater** (under the 1978 Act), NOT the Corporation. This is the single biggest carve-out from the GCC.
- **Urban planning incl. town planning; regulation of land-use and construction of buildings; planning for economic & social development** → **CMDA** (under the TNTCP Act 1971) for the Chennai Metropolitan Area, with the Corporation handling building-plan approval at the local level and DTCP for areas outside CMA.
- **Retained by the Greater Chennai Corporation (1919 Act):** roads & bridges, public health/sanitation/solid-waste management, street lighting, storm-water drains, parks/gardens, burial grounds, public conveniences, registration of births/deaths, markets, regulation of slaughterhouses, and property/profession taxation.
- **Other parastatals** (named for completeness, statutes not in this set): Tamil Nadu Slum Clearance Board (housing-the-poor/slum-upgradation); Chennai Unified Metropolitan Transport Authority / MTC / CMRL (urban transport — a 12th-Schedule item but transport-agency-held); TANGEDCO (electricity, not a 12th-Schedule item).
