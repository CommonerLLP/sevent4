## Notes on the "1978 vs 1979" discrepancy
The statute is commonly cited as the **Gujarat Water Supply and Sewerage Board Act, 1978**, but the enacted/gazetted text bears the number **Gujarat Act No. 18 of 1979** (Presidential assent, first published 6 June 1979). Both the India Code handle title and the gazette copy confirm "18 of 1979." Cite as "1978 (Act 18 of 1979)" to avoid confusion.

## Context bodies (not standalone Ahmedabad statutes — cite only)
- **Electricity:** distribution in Ahmedabad city is by **Torrent Power Ltd** (a licensee, not a statutory body), operating under the **Electricity Act, 2003** (central Act 36 of 2003). India Code: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2058
- **Metro:** built/operated by **Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) Ltd** — a government company, not created by a dedicated state statute; project sanctioned under the central **Metro Railways (Operation and Maintenance) Act, 2002** / Metro policy framework. (cite only; no Ahmedabad-specific statute.)

## Which 12th-Schedule (Art. 243W) functions each law assigns to which body
The Constitution's 12th Schedule lists 18 municipal functions. In Ahmedabad these are split across the statutes above rather than resting entirely with the AMC:

- **GPMC Act, 1949 → Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC).** The bulk of 12th-Schedule functions: public health, sanitation, solid-waste management; water supply (distribution) for domestic/industrial/commercial use; roads & bridges within city; street lighting; registration of births & deaths; regulation of slaughter-houses & tanneries; fire services; public conveniences; markets; building regulation (within AMC limits). These are the obligatory/discretionary duties enumerated in the GPMC Act.
- **GTPUDA, 1976 → AUDA (and the AMC as planning authority within its limits).** The planning-and-land functions: "urban planning including town planning," "regulation of land-use and construction of buildings," and "planning for economic and social development" for the larger Ahmedabad urban development area outside core AMC limits — delivered through the Development Plan and Town Planning Schemes. Within AMC limits the corporation acts as the area's planning authority; AUDA covers the peri-urban development area.
- **GWSSB Act, 1978 (Act 18 of 1979) → GWSSB.** Bulk/regional **water supply** and **sewerage** infrastructure at State scale — upstream of the AMC's last-mile distribution and local sewerage that the GPMC Act assigns to the corporation. (12th-Schedule items: "water supply for domestic, industrial and commercial purposes" and "public health, sanitation conservancy and solid waste management" — the trunk/bulk side.)
- **Not municipal under these Acts:** electricity distribution (Torrent Power under the Electricity Act, 2003) and urban transport/metro (GMRC company). These civic services are deliberately outside the AMC's statutory functions in Ahmedabad.
