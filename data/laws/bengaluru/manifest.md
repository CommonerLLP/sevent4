# Governing Statutes — Bengaluru / Bangalore (Karnataka)

Civic-accountability atlas: authoritative current text of the laws that govern the
administration of Bengaluru. Compiled 2026-06-09. Sources prioritised: India Code
(indiacode.nic.in), Karnataka DPAL, PRS Legislative Research (faithful copies of
India Code / Karnataka Gazette text).

> NOTE ON FLUX: Bengaluru's municipal layer was legally re-founded in 2025. The
> single BBMP has been **dissolved** and replaced by **five City Corporations under a
> Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA)**. See "Current legal status" below. The BBMP Act
> 2020 and KMC Act 1976 are retained here for continuity / transitional reference.

## Which body holds which 12th-Schedule (Art. 243W) function

Bengaluru is the classic "fragmented metropolis": the 18 functions the 74th Amendment's
Twelfth Schedule envisages for a single municipality are split across parastatals:

- **Water supply + sewerage/sanitation (water side)** → **BWSSB** (Act 36 of 1964). Not the corporation.
- **Urban planning incl. town planning, regulation of land-use, planned development** → **BDA** (Act 12 of 1976) as the development authority, operating within the **Karnataka Town & Country Planning Act 1961** (Act 11 of 1963) framework (master plan / RMP). Regional-scale planning sits with BMRDA.
- **Roads, streetlights, solid-waste, public health/sanitation (collection side), local amenities, slums, urban poverty, parks** → the **municipal corporation** — historically BBMP under the **BBMP Act 2020** (and KMC Act 1976 before it), now the **five City Corporations** under the **GBG Act 2025**.
- **Coordination / metropolitan planning across these silos** → the new **Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA)**, intended as the apex coordinator over the corporations and the civic agencies (BWSSB, BDA, BESCOM, BMTC, traffic police, etc.).

This dispersion is the core accountability problem the atlas documents: no single elected
body controls water, land and local services together.

## Current legal status of the Greater Bengaluru reorganisation (as of 2026-06)

- **Enacted.** The Greater Bengaluru Governance Bill 2024 was passed by the Karnataka
  Legislative Assembly (10 Mar 2025), received the Governor's assent, was published in
  the Karnataka Gazette Extraordinary on **24 Apr 2025**, and came **into force on 15 May 2025**
  as **Karnataka Act No. 36 of 2025**.
- **BBMP dissolved.** By government notification the single BBMP was wound up and the
  **GBA + five City Corporations (North, South, East, West, Central)** began functioning
  (reported ~02 Sep 2025). The GBA itself was notified (notifications dated 26 Aug 2025),
  chaired by the Chief Minister, with ~75 members.
- **Structure now in law:** up to **7** City Corporations permitted; currently **5**,
  covering ~27 assembly segments and ~197 wards, under one GBA.
- **Still moving:** the Act has already drawn **amendment bills** — the Greater Bengaluru
  Governance (Amendment) Bill 2025 and a (Second Amendment) Bill 2025 — so the fine print
  (corporation boundaries, ward counts, devolution of staff/assets from BBMP) remains in
  active flux. The BBMP Act 2020 and KMC Act 1976 stay legally relevant for transitional
  provisions, pending litigation, and continuity of liabilities until the transfer is complete.
- **Elections:** as of compilation, fresh corporation elections under the new structure
  were pending/awaited — treat any "elected council" claim for the new corporations as
  unverified until a Karnataka SEC notification confirms polls held.
