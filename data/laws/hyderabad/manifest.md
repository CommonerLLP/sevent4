## Which act actually governs GHMC today

**The Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955 (Act II of 1956)** governs the GHMC — NOT the Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019.

- The corporation originated as the *Hyderabad Municipal Corporation* under the 1955 Act (Act II of 1956). When the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation was formed in 2007 by merging the erstwhile MCH with 12 surrounding municipalities/panchayats, the governing statute was retitled/amended to the **Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955**. The India Code entry and the PDF title page both now read "Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955."
- The Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019, §1(3) states it applies to municipal councils and corporations *"other than the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation"* and "does not apply to the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (constituted under the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955)." So GHMC sits squarely under the 1955 Act; the 2019 Act governs every *other* Telangana ULB.

## 12th-Schedule function → body mapping

Part IX-A / Twelfth Schedule functions for the Hyderabad metro are split across three statutory bodies rather than concentrated in the GHMC:

- **Water supply for domestic, industrial and commercial purposes; sewerage / sewage treatment** (Sch. 12 entries 5–6) → **HMWSSB** (Act 15 of 1989), not GHMC.
- **Urban planning incl. town planning; regulation of land use & construction; planning for economic/social development** (Sch. 12 entries 1–3) at the metropolitan scale → **HMDA** (Act 8 of 2008) — master plans, development permissions, land-pooling over the ~7,257 sq km Hyderabad Metropolitan region. (Local-area building permission is shared with GHMC under its own Act; HMDA Act §8 bars HMDA from ordinarily doing local-body obligatory/discretionary duties except for integrated development.)
- **Remaining municipal civic functions** — roads & bridges, public health/sanitation/solid-waste, street lighting, parks, registration of births/deaths, slum improvement, local taxation, etc. → **GHMC** under the 1955 Act.

## AP → Telangana adoption notes (no fabrication)

- **HMWSSB Act, 1989:** originally an Andhra Pradesh act (assented by the Governor 30 June 1989). The Act in force in the combined State as on 02.06.2014 was **adapted to Telangana under §101 of the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014** (Central Act 6 of 2014) via the **Telangana Adaptation of Laws (No.2) Order, 2016** (G.O.Ms.No.46, Law (F) Dept., dated 01.06.2016). [Stated verbatim on the India Code PDF.]
- **GHMC Act, 1955** and **HMDA Act, 2008:** likewise originate as pre-bifurcation Hyderabad/Andhra Pradesh legislation continued/adapted for Telangana after the 2014 reorganisation; India Code now publishes them under Telangana's location. (Adaptation mechanism = same Telangana Adaptation of Laws Orders under §101 of Act 6 of 2014.)
- **TS Municipalities Act, 2019** is a fresh Telangana enactment (post-bifurcation), replacing the older 1965 municipalities law.

## Notes / caveats

- "Latest amendment" dates are not individually re-verified here; the India Code PDFs are the consolidated/updated versions maintained by Govt of India and include amendment annotations inline (e.g. inserted sections 8-A, 8-B, 9-A, 9-B). Treat the downloaded PDFs as the working consolidated text; for a specific amendment's currency, cross-check the Telangana Gazette.
- The GHMC Act year discrepancy (title "1955", act number "II of **1956**") is genuine and reflected on the official PDF — the bill of 1955 was numbered as Act II of 1956. Not an error.
- Mirror/secondary copies exist on body websites (hmda.gov.in, hyderabadwater.gov.in, GHMC EVDM portal) and PRS/FAOLEX, but India Code is the authoritative source used here.
