# The statutory map of the unelected city

This indexes the actual governing statutes pulled into `data/laws/` (full PDFs are gitignored — public-domain but heavy; the verified citations + source URLs live in each city's `manifest.md`). It answers one question for the atlas: **which law hands which of the 18 Twelfth-Schedule functions to which body — and is that body elected or not?**

All act numbers below were verified by the pulling agents against the statute text itself, not inferred. `verify` caveats are listed at the end.

## The constitutional frame (`_constitution/`)

- **The Constitution (Seventy-fourth Amendment) Act, 1992** — passed 1992, **assent 20 April 1993**, in force **1 June 1993**. Inserted **Part IXA** (Arts 243P–243ZG, "The Municipalities") and the **Twelfth Schedule** (18 functions).
- ★ **The hinge clause — Art. 243W:** the State Legislature **"may, by law, endow"** municipalities with the 18 functions. *Discretionary, not mandatory.* Art. 243ZF gave States one year to pass conformity legislation. → Devolution of the "three Fs" (functions, finances, functionaries) is **State political will, not constitutional compulsion.** This is the legal root of the entire atlas: the scorecard varies by **State law**, not by city, because the Constitution made devolution optional.
- **The 18 functions** (Twelfth Schedule, verbatim list in `_constitution/twelfth-schedule.txt`): 1 urban planning · 2 land-use & building regulation · 3 economic & social development planning · 4 roads & bridges · **5 water supply** · **6 public health, sanitation, conservancy & SWM** · 7 fire services · 8 urban forestry/environment · 9 weaker sections · **10 slum improvement** · **11 urban poverty alleviation** · 12 parks/gardens/playgrounds · 13 cultural/educational/aesthetic · 14 burials & cremations · 15 cattle pounds · 16 vital statistics (births/deaths) · 17 public amenities incl. **street lighting, parking, bus stops** · 18 slaughterhouses & tanneries.

## The cities — statute → body → elected?

Legend: **[elected]** = the directly-elected municipal corporation · **[board]** = state-appointed development authority / parastatal · **[private]** = private firm (context only; not a 12th-Schedule function).

### Ahmedabad (Gujarat)
- **AMC** ← *Gujarat Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949* = **Bombay Act LIX of 1949** **[elected]** — runs water, sewerage, roads, SWM **in-house**.
- **AUDA** ← *Gujarat Town Planning & Urban Development Act, 1976* (Pres. Act 27 of 1976) **[board]** — land / planning.
- **GWSSB** ← *Gujarat Water Supply & Sewerage Board Act* = **Gujarat Act 18 of 1979** [board] — but AMC keeps city water.
- Electricity → **Torrent Power [private]** (Electricity Act 2003). 2023 amendment: Act 17 of 2023 (OBC quota).

### Mumbai (Maharashtra)
- **MCGM / BMC** ← *Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act, 1888* = **Bombay Act III of 1888** **[elected]** — water, sewerage, **BEST** transport, and municipal hospitals **in-house** (the strongest in-house estate in the atlas).
- **MMRDA** ← *MMRDA Act* = **Mah. Act IV of 1975** **[board]** — metropolitan land / infrastructure.
- *Maharashtra Regional & Town Planning Act, 1966* (Mah. XXXVII of 1966) — planning framework.
- *Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act* = **Bombay Act LIX of 1949** — governs Pune/Nagpur etc. (**same statute number as Gujarat's GPMC**).
- Electricity → BEST (BMC, island city) + **Adani Electricity + Tata Power (suburbs) [private]**.

### Kolkata (West Bengal)
- **KMC** ← *Kolkata Municipal Corporation Act, 1980* = **WB Act 59 of 1980** **[elected]** — water, sewerage **in-house**. (Amended: WB Act XVII of 2023.)
- **KMDA** ← *WB Town & Country (Planning & Development) Act, 1979* = **WB Act XIII of 1979** **[board]** — land/planning, **and now the functions of the former Calcutta Improvement Trust.**
- ★★ *Calcutta Improvement Act, 1911* = **Bengal Act V of 1911** (Ghosh's CIT) — **REPEALED**; the CIT was **merged into KMDA w.e.f. 28 April 2017.** The colonial improvement trust *literally became the modern development authority, by statute, in living memory.*
- Electricity → **CESC [private]** (RP-Sanjiv Goenka).

### Chennai (Tamil Nadu)
- **Greater Chennai Corporation** ← *Chennai City Municipal Corporation Act, 1919* = **TN Act IV of 1919** (the **oldest** corporation statute here) **[elected]** — roads, SWM, lighting **in-house**.
- **CMDA** ← *TN Town & Country Planning Act, 1971* = **TN Act 35 of 1972** **[board]** — planning / land.
- **CMWSSB ("Metrowater")** ← *Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply & Sewerage Act, 1978* = **TN Act 28 of 1978** **[board]** — water + sewerage **carved out** of the corporation.
- The 2011 "Greater Chennai" expansion was an **executive notification under the 1919 Act, not a new statute.**

### Bengaluru (Karnataka) — *in active flux*
- ★★ **BBMP DISSOLVED (2025).** *Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024* = **Karnataka Act 36 of 2025** (gazetted 24 Apr 2025, in force 15 May 2025) replaced BBMP with **five City Corporations under a Greater Bengaluru Authority** (functioning ~Sep 2025); **fresh corporation elections are PENDING** — i.e. Bengaluru remains **unelected**. (Superseded the *BBMP Act, 2020* = Karnataka Act 53 of 2020.)
- **BDA** ← *Bangalore Development Authority Act, 1976* = **Karnataka Act 12 of 1976** **[board]** — land/planning (successor to the CITB, 1945).
- **BWSSB** ← *Bangalore Water Supply & Sewerage Act, 1964* = **Karnataka (Mysore) Act 36 of 1964** **[board]** — water + sewerage **carved out**.
- *Karnataka Town & Country Planning Act, 1961* (Act 11 of 1963) — master-plan framework. Electricity → BESCOM [state].

### Hyderabad (Telangana)
- **GHMC** ← *Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955* = **Act II of 1956** **[elected]** — roads, SWM **in-house**. ★ **Explicitly EXEMPTED** from the *Telangana Municipalities Act, 2019* (Act 11 of 2019, §1(3)) — GHMC stays under the 1955 Act.
- **HMDA** ← *Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority Act, 2008* = **Act 8 of 2008** **[board]** — land/planning over **~7,257 km²**.
- **HMWSSB** ← *Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply & Sewerage Act, 1989* = **Act 15 of 1989** **[board]** — water + sewerage **carved out**.
- Metro → **L&T (PPP) [private]**. Electricity → TGSPDCL [state]. (AP acts adapted to Telangana under the 2014 Reorganisation Act.)

## Three things the statutes prove

1. **The Bombay-Presidency lineage is in the act numbers, not just the history.** Gujarat's GPMC Act and Maharashtra's Municipal Corporations Act are *the same statute* — **Bombay Act LIX of 1949**. Mumbai's is older — **Bombay Act III of 1888**. Madras/Chennai older still — **TN Act IV of 1919**. The high-devolution cities run on old, capacious *corporation* acts that keep water in-house.

2. **The improvement-trust → development-authority continuity is now a repealing Act.** The *Calcutta Improvement Act, 1911* (Ghosh's CIT) was repealed and merged into **KMDA** under the 1979 Planning Act, effective **28 April 2017**. What Ghosh reconstructs as a *conceptual* lineage is, in West Bengal, a literal statutory succession.

3. **The carve-out is affirmatively legislated, not a gap.** In every parastatal city, water/sewerage sits in its **own** act creating an unelected board — **CMWSSB Act 1978, BWSSB Act 1964, HMWSSB Act 1989** — and land/planning in another (TN TCP 1971/CMDA, BDA Act 1976, HMDA Act 2008). The elected corporation act governs only the residual. Devolution didn't fail by neglect; the **high-value 12th-Schedule functions (water #5, planning #1–2) were written OUT of the elected body into purpose-built boards.** That is the scorecard, in statute.

## Caveats (verify)
- Some consolidations are dated (GPMC current to 30 Nov 2017; GTPUDA to 2010) — later amendments may not be folded in.
- The CIT-repeal act number (cited as WB Act XIV of 2017) is from secondary sources, **unverified** against the gazette.
- Bengaluru: post-dissolution corporation **elections unconfirmed** — treat any "elected new corporation" claim as pending.
- Gujarat Water Board: brief said "1978"; the enacted/gazetted text is **Act 18 of 1979** (flagged).
