# The Unelected City: who profits from Ahmedabad's land

*A follow-the-money dossier. Every claim is tiered: **(A)** documented fact, **(B)** reported allegation (attributed, with denials, never asserted as fact), **(C)** analysis of the mechanism. The documentable core is the lawful land-value-capture machine — not any crime.*

## The machine: AUDA's town planning scheme (A + C)

The engine of Ahmedabad's western expansion is not a market; it is a statute. Under the **Gujarat Town Planning & Urban Development Act, 1976**, the **Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority (AUDA)** runs the **Town Planning Scheme (TPS)** — land pooling and "reconstitution." AUDA pools every parcel in a fringe area, deducts up to ~40% of each owner's land (for roads, parks, amenities *and* a saleable public reserve), returns ~60% as serviced "final plots" worth far more than the raw farmland, and then **auctions the land it kept at the new, infrastructure-bearing value** — plus a *betterment charge* of one-half the value increase on the returned plots **(A)**. Ahmedabad is the first Indian city to deliver ~90% of its Development Plan through TPS, and TPS land sales are a core AUDA revenue stream (WRI; Lincoln Institute / Ballaney). This is exactly **Ghosh's "recoupment"** — the colonial improvement trust that financed itself by selling surplus land it had acquired and improved, at a value "rendered from the future" — running a century on. **AUDA is the improvement trust reborn (C).**

The same TPS machine assembled the **76-km Sardar Patel (SP) Ring Road** (built by AUDA, ~Rs 355 cr, opened 2004) **(A)**. Draw the ring, service the catchment, and the fringe re-prices.

## The western periphery: who captures the uplift (A)

The gain flows to whoever holds title when an area is notified. TPS lets **only registered landowners** participate in scheme design; renters, informal settlers and the landless are excluded from the process *and* the windfall (WRI) **(A)**. Large fringe landholders surrender ~40% but keep ~60% as serviced plots whose value multiplies. Then come the **developers and aggregators** along the SP Ring Road / SG Highway / Bopal–South Bopal–Shela–Shilaj corridor and the Sanand-toward-GIFT axis. The price record is documented: SP Ring Road West apartments at ~Rs 5,950/sq ft in 2026, up **~+43% over five years**; Shela/South Bopal ~Rs 4,500–6,000/sq ft; SG Highway/Thaltej/Satellite Rs 6,000+/sq ft (99acres, May-2026) **(A)**. The publicly-funded infrastructure uplift is privately captured by anticipation — **Cooper's asset-price inflation as the real engine of accumulation (C)**.

## The Sabarmati Riverfront: manufacturing land (A)

**SRFDCL**, an AMC special-purpose vehicle, narrowed the river to reclaim **~202.79 ha of brand-new, central, high-value land**, earmarking ~14% for commercial/residential sale; in 2020 it appointed **JLL India to monetise ~500 acres valued at ~Rs 3,500 crore** **(A)**. Its capital logic — create new land from a public river, sell the prime fraction to fund the whole — is Ghosh's recoupment in its purest form. The debit side: a 2003 survey counted **14,555 slum households** on the bank, ~6,293 assessed as needing rehabilitation; reporting describes **>10,000 residents displaced** and inadequate, deteriorating resettlement (Down To Earth; Land Conflict Watch) **(A)**. Central riverbank land was converted from informal-settlement use to saleable frontage; the uplift accrued upward, the displacement cost fell downward **(C)**.

## GIFT City: the state as financial-zone landlord (A)

**GIFT City** began as a 50:50 JV between Gujarat and IL&FS. When IL&FS collapsed, **GUDCL bought out its 50% stake for ~Rs 32 crore equity (June 2020)**, giving the Gujarat government full ownership of **GIFTCL** and its 100%-subsidiary **GIFT SEZ Ltd** (The Tribune; The Wire) **(A)**. The state-as-landlord then allots built-up area in a tax-privileged IFSC: **Brigade** ~1.1M sq ft (2014), **Hiranandani** ~0.25M sq ft (its "Signature" tower is the IFSC's first fully functional commercial tower), residential parcels to Savvy/Shivalik et al., and 20+ financial institutions — ~50% of GIFT allotted by late 2025 **(A)**. The public assembles and services the land; private allottees enjoy the location rent and the SEZ/IFSC tax regime — **Cooper's privatization of a state-built public good (C)**.

## The mega-event land machine (A)

*Clarification: the Commonwealth Games were **Delhi 2010**, not Ahmedabad.* Ahmedabad's real pipeline is the **2036 Olympic bid** (India's lead candidate; IOC "Continuous Dialogue" from Aug 2025), the National Games build-out, and G20 (2023) venues. The event re-zones the northwest: the **SVP Sports Enclave (~355 acres, Motera**, by the Narendra Modi Stadium), the **Karai Sports Hub (~143 acres**, partly on reclaimed riverfront), and a Naranpura enclave, with construction from 2026 **(A)**. A mega-event legitimises assembling hundreds of acres and pouring public infrastructure into the periphery, spiking surrounding values — **Cooper's "extravagance" (spectacle spending) fused with Ghosh's recoupment (C)**.

## The "black money / drugs" angle — strictly as reported (B)

- **Periphery black money (B):** benami (no-name) holdings and large unaccounted **cash components** are *reported* as a pattern for parking black money in fast-appreciating, opaquely-valued fringe land. This is **not asserted as fact against any named person**. Among ED actions touching Ahmedabad-area land, the **ED has attached land parcels in Sanand** in proceedings against the **Reliance Anil Ambani (ADAG)** group (Business Standard); ADAG entities generally contest ED proceedings and assert compliance.
- **Mundra Port heroin (B):** On **16 Sept 2021 the DRI seized ~2,988 kg of heroin** (origin Afghanistan, via Bandar Abbas, Iran) from two containers at **Adani-operated Mundra Port** — among India's largest such seizures (Business Today; Al Jazeera). Commentators including former MP **Jawhar Sircar** and **Adani Watch** publicly questioned why Mundra was used and urged a government takeover. **Adani's stated position:** it is a port operator with **"no policing authority over containers"** — under the Customs Act only Customs/DRI may open or seize cargo — and it announced it would stop handling cargo from Afghanistan, Iran and Pakistan (Nov 2021) (Business Standard). **No finding has tied Adani Ports to the contraband, no charge has followed, and no link between this seizure and Ahmedabad land laundering is asserted or sourced here.**

## The verdict (C)

Read through Ghosh and Cooper, Ahmedabad's land story is not scandal but **structure**: a 1976 statute that lets an *unelected* authority manufacture, improve and sell the city's land, distributing the uplift to titleholders, developers and a tax-privileged financial zone, while the displaced bear the cost. *Badly run for whom — and profitably run for whom?*
