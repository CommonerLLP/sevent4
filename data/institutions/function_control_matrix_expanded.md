# The expanded control matrix: the 18 functions across the three tiers (Union · State · City)

For each of the **18 Twelfth-Schedule functions** (Article 243W), this names the actual institution at each
tier — the **Union of India** body/scheme that overlays it, the **State** board/authority/department that
holds it, and the **City** (elected corporation) department that runs it — and marks **who actually
controls it** in each of the six cities.

**How to read it.** India's Constitution is a **two-tier fiscal federation** (Union + State). The 74th
Amendment added the city as a *third tier administratively, never fiscally*. So across all 18 functions the
pattern repeats: the **Union sets the scheme and holds the money** (it funds through missions and the
Finance Commission), the **State owns the body** (it legislates the board/authority that holds the
function), and the **City is left to operate the residual** — and to pay. Bold marks the controlling tier.

> Sources: `service_providers.json`, `fragmentation/`, `data/laws/`. Union schemes are run by the **Ministry
> of Housing & Urban Affairs (MoHUA)** unless noted. `‡` = exact body/year flagged `verify` in the JSONs.

### 1 · Urban planning, including town planning
- **Union:** MoHUA (national urban policy); Smart Cities Mission; AMRUT.
- **State [controls]:** the development authority — **AUDA** (Ahd) · **MMRDA** (Mum) · **KMDA** (Kol) · **CMDA** (Che) · **BDA/BMRDA** (Blr) · **HMDA** (Hyd). All created by *state* Acts.
- **City:** the corporation's town-planning wing (advisory only).
- **Controls:** **State**, all six.

### 2 · Regulation of land-use & construction of buildings
- **Union:** MoHUA model building bye-laws.
- **State [land-use]:** the same development authority owns the *master plan / zoning*.
- **City [permits]:** the corporation issues *building permits* within that plan (AMC; MCGM under DCPR 2034; GHMC via TS-bPASS; etc.).
- **Controls:** **Mixed** — plan = State, permit = City. (The decisive power, land-use, is the State's.)

### 3 · Planning for economic & social development
- **Union:** NITI Aayog; MoHUA.
- **State [controls]:** the **District Planning Committee** (Art. 243ZD) and **Metropolitan Planning Committee** (Art. 243ZE) — constitutionally mandated but **largely unconstituted or state-run** in every metro here.
- **City:** nominal representation only.
- **Controls:** **State** (the MPC, where it exists at all, is not the elected corporation).

### 4 · Roads & bridges
- **Union:** MoRTH / NHAI (national highways through the city).
- **State:** state PWD + development authority (arterials, flyovers, ring roads).
- **City [controls]:** the corporation runs the **local road network** — AMC, MCGM, KMC, GCC, BBMP, GHMC.
- **Controls:** **City** (local) — but the big roads are State/Union.

### 5 · Water supply (domestic, industrial, commercial)
- **Union:** Ministry of Jal Shakti / CPHEEO; **AMRUT** & **Jal Jeevan Mission–Urban** (funds).
- **State:** the water board — **CMWSSB** (Che, 1978) · **BWSSB** (Blr, 1964) · **HMWSSB** (Hyd, 1989).
- **City:** the corporation's water dept — **AMC** (Ahd) · **MCGM** (Mum) · **KMC** (Kol).
- **Controls:** **City** in Ahmedabad / Mumbai / Kolkata · **State** in Chennai / Bengaluru / Hyderabad. *(The single biggest swing function.)*

### 6 · Public health, sanitation, conservancy & solid-waste management
- **Union:** **Swachh Bharat Mission–Urban**; Min. of Health.
- **State:** state public-health dept (oversight); SWM Rules, 2016 (the framework).
- **City [controls]:** the corporation — AMC, MCGM, KMC, GCC, **BBMP/BSWML**, GHMC. *Operation is heavily contracted* (Urbaser/Ramky etc.) and the workforce is contract-Dalit labour.
- **Controls:** **City** (nominal); the *operation* drifts to contractors.

### 7 · Fire services
- **Union:** MHA / Directorate-General Fire Services (advisory; fire is a State subject).
- **State:** **WB Fire & Emergency** (Kol) · **TN Fire & Rescue** (Che) · **Karnataka Fire & Emergency** (Blr) · **Telangana Fire Services** (Hyd).
- **City:** **AMC Fire & Emergency** (Ahd) · **Mumbai Fire Brigade–BMC** (Mum).
- **Controls:** **City** only in Ahmedabad / Mumbai · **State** in the other four.

### 8 · Urban forestry, protection of the environment, ecology
- **Union:** MoEFCC; **CPCB**.
- **State [controls]:** the **State Pollution Control Board** (GPCB / MPCB / WBPCB / TNPCB / KSPCB / TGPCB) regulates; the state forest dept holds reserved greens.
- **City:** the corporation does street-tree / urban-greening.
- **Controls:** **Mixed**, State-led (regulation is the SPCB's).

### 9 · Safeguarding the interests of weaker sections (incl. disabled)
- **Union:** Min. of Social Justice & Empowerment (DEPwD).
- **State [controls]:** state social-welfare / disability departments.
- **City:** minor welfare schemes.
- **Controls:** **State**.

### 10 · Slum improvement & upgradation
- **Union:** **PMAY–Urban** (housing); DAY-NULM.
- **State [controls]:** the slum/housing board — **SRA + MHADA** (Mum) · **TN Urban Habitat Dev Board / TNSCB** (Che) · **Karnataka Slum Dev Board** (Blr) · **MEPMA / Telangana** (Hyd) · Gujarat slum cell + state (Ahd) · WB (Kol).
- **City:** implementation partner at best.
- **Controls:** **State**.

### 11 · Urban poverty alleviation
- **Union [controls — designs]:** **DAY-NULM** (Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana–National Urban Livelihoods Mission) — a *central* scheme.
- **State:** the State Urban Livelihoods Mission implements it.
- **City:** a city livelihoods unit (e.g. SUDA, MEPMA) delivers.
- **Controls:** **Centre** (designed and funded centrally; the city is a delivery arm).

### 12 · Provision of urban amenities — parks, gardens, playgrounds
- **Union:** —
- **State:** state horticulture holds the marquee gardens (**Lalbagh / Cubbon**, Blr).
- **City [controls]:** neighbourhood parks — the corporation.
- **Controls:** **City** (the big botanical parks excepted).

### 13 · Promotion of cultural, educational & aesthetic aspects
- **Union:** Min. of Education / Culture.
- **State:** the state education dept (the bulk of schooling).
- **City:** corporation-run **municipal schools** (BMC, AMC, KMC, GCC).
- **Controls:** **Mixed**, State-led.

### 14 · Burials & burial grounds; cremations & crematoriums
- **Union:** —
- **State:** —
- **City [controls]:** the corporation.
- **Controls:** **City**.

### 15 · Cattle pounds; prevention of cruelty to animals
- **Union:** Animal Welfare Board of India (advisory).
- **State:** state animal-husbandry dept (rules).
- **City [controls]:** the corporation runs the pounds.
- **Controls:** **City**.

### 16 · Vital statistics, including registration of births & deaths
- **Union:** **Registrar-General of India** — the Civil Registration System under the Births & Deaths Registration Act, 1969 (amended 2023) sets the framework.
- **State:** the state Chief Registrar.
- **City [controls]:** the corporation is the local Registrar — it actually registers.
- **Controls:** **City** (operates), within a Union framework.

### 17 · Public amenities — street lighting, parking lots, bus stops, public conveniences
- **Union:** —
- **State:** the **bus service** is a state transport corporation — **MTC** (Che) · **BMTC** (Blr) · **TSRTC** (Hyd) · **WBTC** (Kol). *(Exception: **BEST** is a Mumbai-BMC undertaking — City.)*
- **City:** street lighting, parking, public toilets — the corporation.
- **Controls:** **Mixed** — lighting/parking = City, *transport* = State (except Mumbai).

### 18 · Regulation of slaughterhouses & tanneries
- **Union:** FSSAI (meat-safety standards).
- **State:** state pollution board (tannery effluent).
- **City [controls]:** the corporation runs municipal slaughterhouses.
- **Controls:** **City**.

## The tally — and what the three tiers tell you

| City | Fully **City** | **Mixed** | **State** | **Centre** | *Not the elected city* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ahmedabad / Mumbai | **9 / 18** | 4 | 4 | 1 | 9 (5 state+centre, 4 mixed) |
| Kolkata | **8 / 18** | 4 | 5 | 1 | 10 |
| Chennai / Bengaluru / Hyderabad | **7 / 18** | 4 | 6 | 1 | 11 |

Read down the three tiers and the division of labour is unmistakable:
- **The Union holds the money and the mission.** It rarely *runs* anything, but it funds (AMRUT, Smart Cities, Swachh Bharat, PMAY, DAY-NULM, Jal Jeevan) and conditions (the 15th Finance Commission's ₹4.36-lakh-crore grant, tied to property-tax floors). Control through the purse.
- **The State owns the bodies.** Every carve-out — planning, water, slums, fire, transport, environment — is a *state* Act creating a *state* board. The state is where the city's powers went.
- **The City is left the residual** — the seven labour-heavy, low-rent functions (sanitation, local roads, parks, burials, cattle, registration, slaughterhouses) — **and no power to tax** (no Seventh-Schedule entry; Art. 243X's "may authorise"). Few functions, no fiscal base.

This is the website's core explainer: **the Union pays-and-conditions, the State owns-and-decides, and the City you elect is handed the broom and the bill.**
